# GeoGuide: Interview Guide

Purpose: collect evidence that the problem is real and that the planned features are useful (relevance evidence for the diploma project).

Format: a conversation of 10 to 15 minutes with 3 to 5 people (tourists, people who recently traveled, tour guides).

Rules:
- Ask open questions and let the person talk. Follow up with "Why?", "Can you give an example?", "What happened then?".
- Do not present GeoGuide before question 6.
- Write down what people actually say, including negative answers. Do not edit answers to fit the project.

---

## Introduction (read aloud)

"Hello, I am a student at Astana IT University working on a diploma project about a tourist guide application. I would like to ask you a few questions about how you get information when you travel. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes, there are no right or wrong answers. May I take notes? Is it okay to mention your answers in the project without your name?"

## Core questions

**Current experience**
1. Tell me about your last trip to a new city. How did you find out about the places you visited?
2. When you see a building or monument and want to know what it is, what do you do? How well does that work?
3. What is the most annoying part of getting information about places when you travel? Can you give an example?
4. Have you used a guide, an audio guide, or a travel app? What was good and what was bad?
5. Have you used tools like Google Lens or an AI chatbot for travel questions? How did it go, and did you trust the answers?

**Reaction to the idea** (now describe GeoGuide in 2-3 sentences, show 3-4 screens of the prototype if you can)

> "GeoGuide is a mobile app. You point the phone camera at a building and it explains what it is. You can ask an AI guide questions in a chat. When you arrive near an interesting place, you get a notification, and tapping it opens information about the place. It also recommends places based on your interests."

6. What do you think about this idea? Would you use it on a trip? Why or why not?
7. Which part would be most useful for you, and which part would you not use?
8. What would make you not trust it, or what would worry you (wrong answers, notifications, sharing your location)?

**Closing**
9. Is there anything missing, or anything you would add?

---

## Prompts to use if the person has little to say

- Notifications: "How do you feel about getting a notification on your phone when you are near an interesting place?"
- Location: "How do you feel about sharing your location with a travel app?"
- Languages: "In which language would you want the information?"
- Internet: "Would you need it to work without internet?"
