## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, cultured, and verified travel aesthetic tailored to regional exploration, fieldwork, and regional discovery. Drawing inspiration from modern high-end editorial journalism and field atlases, the visual identity pairs the disciplined calm of print documentation with responsive, modern digital utility.

The core visual style is an **Editorial Neo-Minimalism**:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation**: The interface rejects clinical pure whites in favor of warm, tactile paper surfaces (`#f6f6f3`) grounded with rich obsidian ink (`#0e1a2b`).
- **Cartographic Precision**: Restrained structural hairline dividers, generous whitespace, clear factual hierarchies, and micro-labeling reinforce factual reliability.
- **Atmospheric Immersion**: Visual drama stems from natural landscape photography, dynamic camera overlays, and targeted accent marks rather than synthetic gradients or excessive ornament.
- **Factual Transparency**: Sourced callouts, verified fact indicators, and confidence tags receive bespoke structural framing to anchor credibility.

## Colors

The palette mimics a high-grade print publication layered over tactical navigation tools:

- **Primary (`#0e1a2b`)**: Deep mineral ink. Anchors the visual hierarchy, applied to dominant typographic headlines, primary interactive CTA backgrounds, active system tabs, and prominent scanning controls.
- **Secondary (`#0d5c8f`)**: Steppe blue. Used intentionally for editorial subtitles, region kickers, contextual map links, secondary action texts, and interactive form states.
- **Tertiary (`#b9892c`)**: Field gold. Reserved for authoritative indicators, citation bars, verified provenance callouts, and location highlight badges.
- **Neutral (`#f6f6f3`)**: Unbleached natural paper. The pervasive canvas tone across all primary content views, reducing glare and lending physical substance.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Surface Elevation**: Canvas starts at `#f6f6f3`, while cards, bottom sheets, and elevated overlays shift to `#ffffff` to create distinct dimensional separation. Dark spatial states (Scanner, Reticle overlays) shift to `#0b1422` with varying degrees of translucent obsidian scrims.
- **Text Ranks**: Primary body `#0e1a2b`, long-form narrative body `#2b394d`, contextual metadata and input placeholders `#46546a`, and structural captions `#5b687b`.
- **Informational Badging**: Paired soft backgrounds with deep chromatic text—Nature blue (`#e6eef5` / `#0a4a74`), verified status (`#e1f3e8` / `#0d5a2f`), and advisory/rare alerts (`#f3ead4` / `#6a4b0d`).

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Manrope**, leveraging its geometric bones, open apertures, and robust weight distribution to move seamlessly from commanding editorial titles to dense, legible field notes.

- **Editorial Hierarchy**: Display and headline tiers run heavy (`font-weight: 800`) with tight tracking to echo classic broadsheet headers and guidebooks.
- **Readable Long-Form**: Body copy is set at generous line-height multiples (`1.55` to `1.6`) to maintain effortless scanning during outdoor field reading.
- **Systematic Metadata**: Eyebrows, region tags, and section headers strictly deploy uppercase transformations with wide tracking (`0.08em` - `0.1em`) at small scales (`11px` - `12.5px`), instantly signaling structured categorization.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced fluid container model optimized for field utility:

- **Canvas Insets & Margins**: Standard content canvas utilizes a 20px (`1.25rem`) side margin. Onboarding and open-canvas displays expand to 28px (`1.75rem`) to frame hero visuals, while contextual tools (chat message streams, map drawers) contract to 16px (`1rem`) to maximize space.
- **Rhythm & Stacking**: Vertical progression relies on an 8-point base increment:
  - Tight micro-pairings (labels, indicators, subheads): `4px` - `8px` (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).
  - Inter-component flow (lists, text blocks, stat cells): `14px` - `16px` (`space-md`).
  - Major section breaks: `20px` - `28px` (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- **Screen Form Factors**:
  - **Mobile (< 430px)**: Single-column edge-to-edge flow with fixed 84px bottom utility bars and 48px status offsets.
  - **Tablet & Desktop (>= 430px)**: Interfaces center within calibrated reading modules (max mobile viewport 390px-480px, expanded tablet split-views spanning a dual 12-column asymmetric layout where map and media occupy 7 columns and editorial dossiers occupy 5 columns).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines crisp tactile borders with soft ambient drop shadows reserved solely for spatial layering:

- **Flat Editorial Surface Separation**: Base levels sit flat. Structural divisions on the paper canvas rely primarily on hairline borders (`1px solid #e3e6ea`) rather than box shadows.
- **Ambient Field Elevation**: When navigation bars, bottom sheets, or interactive cards hover over spatial contexts (such as real-time maps or photographic camera viewports), they employ diffused, tinted drop shadows:
  - **Floating Tooling & Search**: `0 2px 10px rgba(14, 26, 43, 0.12)`
  - **Bottom Inspection Sheets**: `0 6px 24px rgba(14, 26, 43, 0.18)`
  - **Critical Floating Alerts**: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`
- **Glassmorphic Camera Controls**: Active camera controls and floating image headers utilize translucent backdrops (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) framed with refined ghost borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), ensuring optical legibility against variable landscape photography.

## Shapes

The shape system employs deliberate variation to distinguish between structural content containers and dynamic interactive triggers:

- **Standard Cards & Modules (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`)**: Content panels, metric boxes, and list items use 14px to 18px radii, creating an organized, architectural structure.
- **Sheets & Modals (`rounded-xl`)**: Top and bottom slide-in sheets deploy soft, pronounced shoulders (22px to 26px) to communicate physical layering.
- **Pills (`rounded-full` / `99px`)**: Interactive tags, region chips, prompt queries, segmented indicator thumbs, and floating status indicators are fully radiused pills.
- **Asymmetric Elements**: Real-time AI chat bubbles utilize directional corner pinning: user bubbles taper at the bottom-right (`18px 18px 4px 18px`), while system responses taper at the bottom-left (`18px 18px 18px 4px`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#0e1a2b`, text `#ffffff`, height 52px, border radius 16px, `font-weight: 700`. Active click shifts lightness slightly with no border.
- **Secondary Outlines**: Transparent background with `1.5px solid #0e1a2b` border, text `#0e1a2b`, height 44px to 48px, border radius 15px.
- **Center Action Button**: Elevated 58px circular floating scanner trigger (`#0e1a2b`) positioned with a negative margin breakout on the bottom navigation dock.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Active State**: Background `#0e1a2b`, text `#ffffff`, hairline border transparent, padding 8px 16px, fully radiused.
- **Inactive State**: Background `#ffffff`, text `#46546a`, border `1px solid #cfd5dd`, padding 8px 16px, fully radiused.

### Cards & Media Panels
- **Editorial Cards**: Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e3e6ea`, border radius 18px, padding 16px. Internal media thumbnails clipped to 12px border radius.
- **Hero Image Cards**: Full-bleed photographic container with 24px border radius and bottom linear scrim (`rgba(8, 18, 34, 0)` to `rgba(8, 18, 34, 0.82)`) anchoring white typography.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs**: Fill `#ffffff` (or `#f6f6f3` when embedded in sheets), border `1.5px solid #cfd5dd`, corner radius 16px, text `#0e1a2b`, placeholder `#46546a`. Focused state shifts border to `#0d5c8f`.

### Form Controls (Toggles, Radios, Segmented Bars)
- **Segmented Control**: Recessed track `#eef0f3`, inner container padding 3px, corner radius 14px. Active segment pill `#ffffff` with `0 1px 4px rgba(14,26,43,.15)` shadow.
- **Toggle Switch**: Track height 28px, width 48px. Inactive `#cfd5dd`, active fill `#0d5c8f`. Thumb `#ffffff` with 2px inset.

### Editorial Callouts (Verified Facts)
- **Fact Blocks**: Surface `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e3e6ea`, rounded 14px, featuring a dedicated `3px solid #b9892c` left accent rule. Sourced citations displayed below in uppercase `11px` metadata (`#46546a`).
